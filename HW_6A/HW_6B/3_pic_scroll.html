Year 2008

These three images show Navy Yard, a neighborhood in Washington, DC, in 2008, 2012, 2016. As you compare the images, you might notice that significant construction over the course of this 8 year period has altered the landscape. Notably, 2008 and 2016 have more portions of emply plots of land, whereas the vast majority of the area was covered in buildings in 2012. Notice, also, that a walkway appears to have been build by the water between 2008 and 2012. The streets and general layout of the neighborhood, on the other hand, remain nearly exactly the same.
